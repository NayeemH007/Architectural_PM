# Risks & Mitigations

> **Working name:** PracticeLens (placeholder). **Purpose of this document:** a decision-ready risk register for the single-firm Dhaka pilot (~6 months) and its productization path, with a concrete, build-time mitigation for every risk — not platitudes. We rate **Likelihood** and **Impact** on a 1–5 scale (5 = near-certain / existential), assign an **Owner** (a real role on a small startup team, not a committee), and state the **Residual risk** that remains *after* the mitigation is in place. The register closes with the **top-5 must-not-fail risks** and the early-warning signals we will instrument from day one.
>
> **Framing principle:** this product's central bet is that the highest-value data is *uncaptured* (WhatsApp, phone, paper, the principal's head). Almost every risk below is a variant of "the data isn't there, isn't trustworthy, or won't keep flowing." So our risk posture is not "prevent all bad data" — it is **make missingness and low confidence measurable and visible** (`Data Completeness Score`, per-record `confidence`, provenance, refuse-on-missing) so the product degrades honestly instead of lying. A risk register for an analytics layer is really a register of *trust failures*.
>
> **Owner legend:** **CEO/Founder** (commercial, pilot relationship, fundraising), **CTO** (architecture, security, AI, integrations), **PE** (product engineer — connectors, pipeline, dashboards), **PM/IC** (product manager doubling as implementation consultant / customer success on the pilot), **Champion** (the pilot firm's owner/principal — our internal sponsor).

---

## 1. How to read the ratings

| Score | Likelihood | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rare | Cosmetic / easily absorbed |
| 2 | Unlikely | Minor rework |
| 3 | Plausible | Hurts a milestone or a KPI's credibility |
| 4 | Likely | Threatens pilot success or a core promise |
| 5 | Near-certain | Existential / kills the pilot or the company |

**Exposure = Likelihood × Impact.** Anything with Exposure ≥ 12 *or* Impact = 5 is a candidate for the must-not-fail list in §4.

## 2. The 14 mandated risks

| 1 | **Inconsistent project names** — the same `Project` appears as five different strings across Drive folders, Tally ledgers, Excel registers, WhatsApp threads (Bangla + Latin, "Karim Sb flat", "Banani 6-storey", a Tally ledger code). Bad matching = wrong rollups = wrong margins. | 5 | 4 | `ProjectCrossReference` + `ProjectAlias` is a **first-class engineering subsystem, not a cleanup step**: Unicode NFC + Bijoy→Unicode normalization, honorific/suffix stripping, transliteration, blocking + fuzzy scoring → **human-confirmed match UI** (suggested matches, one-click confirm). This is the single biggest blind spot in a small Dhaka firm. | 5 | 5 | **The platform helps *create* the data, it does not assume it.** Ship a **2-tap weekly effort-capture** (per `Employee` × `Project` × phase, day or half-day granularity — not 6-minute increments) via web + WhatsApp-style mobile form. Until timesheets exist, **People KPIs render as "insufficient data — start capture here" and Money KPIs that need labour cost show fee/cash-only margin with an explicit "labour cost not yet captured" badge.** Never fabricate a margin. | 3 | **Poor data quality** — typos, stale rows, duplicate clients, half-finished captures, dates in dd/mm vs mm/dd, BDT vs lakh/crore notation. | 4 | 4 | Quality is **modelled, not assumed clean**: every `IntegrationRecord` carries `confidence`; KPI builders propagate the **minimum** input confidence; `Data Completeness Score` per `Project`/domain is shown on every dashboard header. Validation at ingest (date format, currency, required-field, range checks) → rejects to a **review queue**, not silent drop. Detectors **refuse to fire below a per-capability confidence threshold**. | CTO | Plausible-but-wrong values that pass validation (a real but mistyped fee) still flow; mitigated by "Why this number?" drill-down letting the Champion spot-check. |

For the firm's *legacy* sheets, a **mapping wizard** (column-to-field drag-map, saved as a reusable `DataSource` profile per sheet) + preview-before-commit. Reject ambiguous parses to the review queue. | PE | Firms drift off-template over time; one-off historical sheets need bespoke mapping each time → bounded by the saved-profile mechanism. |
| 5 | **API restrictions** — rate limits, quota/pricing changes, thin webhooks, no history backfill. Treat MVP integration surface as {templated import + Google + Microsoft + email/calendar + manual capture}; rich APIs (Tally/QBO/Xero/APS) are post-MVP, opportunistic. | CTO | A vendor can still break or re-price an adapter overnight; residual contained to that one source's freshness, surfaced via per-`DataSource` "last synced" + completeness. |
| 6 | **Employee resistance** — staff see "tracking software" / surveillance; principals fear effort data exposing under-pricing; juniors resent extra clicks. Adoption collapse kills the pilot. | 4 | 5 | **Design for the principal's pain first, the junior's effort last.** (a) Money/Delivery dashboards deliver owner value with **zero new staff data entry** (they ride on Excel/Drive/email/manual-by-owner capture) — so the product is useful *before* anyone is asked to log time. (b) Effort capture is opt-in-framed as "so we can price the next job right," not surveillance; **no per-person productivity leaderboards in the pilot**; finance data restricted to Owner/Finance per RBAC. (c) 2-tap weekly capture, WhatsApp-channel reminders, Champion endorses publicly. | PM/IC | Cultural resistance to *any* measurement persists in some firms; residual = slower People-domain rollout, not pilot failure (decoupled by design). |
| 7 | **Privacy concerns** — client/financial data + bilingual PII; PDPO 2025 (enacted Nov 2025) + 2026 amendment (risk-based localization); cross-border transfer of *sensitive* identifiers (NID/TIN/passport) needs prior govt approval; enforcement ~May 2027. | 3 | 5 | **Data minimization + region-selectable residency by design.** Default cloud region Singapore/Mumbai; **segregate sensitive identifiers (NID/TIN/passport)** into a tagged store that can be kept in a **Bangladesh local mirror** and excluded from cross-border flows. Avoid collecting sensitive PII unless a KPI needs it (it mostly doesn't). Per-tenant encryption keys, consent capture, `AuditLog` on every access, DPA + data-processing terms with the pilot firm. Track PDPO enforcement timeline. | CTO | Regulatory ambiguity (sensitive-data definition, CII classification) until ~2027 enforcement; residual = compliance re-work if rules tighten, bounded by minimization. |
| 8 | **Incorrect KPI interpretation** — owner reads `Utilization Rate` 60% as "lazy staff" when timesheet coverage is 40%; treats `Forecast Project Margin` as fact when it's an estimate on thin data; misreads `Invoice Aging` that excludes WhatsApp-agreed deferrals. | 4 | 4 | **Every KPI tile is self-documenting.** "Why this number?" exposes source rows + dates + formula + `calc_version` + **confidence badge** + the `Data Completeness Score` that produced it. KPIs with low completeness render **greyed with a coverage caveat**, never as a clean number. Ship a one-page **KPI dictionary in-product** (definition + what it does/doesn't include). Weekly AI report states assumptions and refuses claims the data can't support. | PM/IC | Owners may still over-trust a confident-looking number; residual reduced by mandatory caveat banners under any KPI with completeness < threshold. |
| 9 | **AI hallucinations** — the AI report invents a margin, a missed milestone, or an approval date; cites a source that doesn't say that; produces fluent-but-false narrative the owner acts on. | 4 | 5 | **Anti-hallucination is architectural, not promptcraft.** KPIs and anomalies are computed by **deterministic detectors over the gold layer**; the LLM only *narrates pre-computed, cited facts* — it never produces a number. Every AI claim carries **source record IDs + dates + calculation logic + confidence**; **refuse-on-missing** ("insufficient data to assess margin"). Grounding-only generation, no free-form metric invention. Citations are clickable and validated to exist. | CTO | LLM can still mis-phrase or over-generalize a correctly-sourced fact; residual mitigated by template-constrained narration + Champion review of the first weeks' reports. |
| 10 | **Integration failures** — a connector silently stops (token expired, folder permission revoked, Tally HTTP server off, WhatsApp webhook 5×-failed-and-disabled), dashboards look fine but go stale → owner makes decisions on old data. | 4 | 4 | **Freshness is a first-class, monitored signal.** Per-`DataSource` heartbeat + "last synced" timestamp on every dashboard domain; **staleness > SLA degrades `Data Completeness Score` and raises an alert** to PM/IC + Champion. **Persist every raw webhook before processing** (mandatory for WhatsApp — no replay/backfill from Meta). Dead-letter queue + retry with alerting. No silent failures: a dead connector turns its tiles amber. | PE | Detection lag between failure and SLA breach; residual minimized by short SLAs on critical sources (finance, manual capture). |
| 11 | **BIM complexity** — pressure to ingest Revit/APS/Archicad model data; high effort (async translation, OAuth scopes, Dec-2025 consumption pricing) for a market that is mostly AutoCAD 2D + SketchUp with **no practice-data API**. | 2 | 3 | **Explicitly out of MVP scope.** Treat AutoCAD/SketchUp/D5 as **file-presence signals only** (existence, name, size, last-modified, folder) captured via the *cloud file store* (Drive/OneDrive/SharePoint), used as a proxy for `Deliverable`/`Drawing` activity — **not** via the CAD tool. APS/Archicad/BIMcloud are **pluggable, post-MVP, productization-tier** connectors for richer markets. Resist scope creep. | CTO | A pilot stakeholder may demand model-level data; residual = a "no" backed by the file-presence proxy delivering 80% of the signal at 5% of the cost. |
| 12 | **Different architecture workflows** — phase models, billing triggers, approval gates and roles vary firm-to-firm; a hard-coded RIBA/AIA flow won't fit the next firm. | 3 | 3 | **Config-driven, multi-tenant-ready from day one.** Phase model (Concept→Schematic→DD→Authority Approval→CD→Tender→CA→Handover) is a **per-`Company` template**, not hard-code; `Service` lines, `Milestone`/`Approval` types, and `Fee` triggers are configurable. Canonical entities/KPIs are fixed vocabulary; their *instantiation* is tenant config. Pilot validates the config surface against one real firm before generalizing. | CTO | Over-configuration could slow onboarding later; residual managed by shipping sensible Dhaka defaults so most firms tweak rather than build. |
| 13 | **Local accounting variations** — Tally vs QBO vs Xero vs Excel vs bank statements; VAT 15%, **VDS** withheld at source, **AIT/TDS ~10% resident / ~20% non-resident** reduce *cash collected*; lakh/crore notation; multi-currency. | 4 | 4 | **Model the BD withholding flow explicitly.** `Invoice`/`Payment`/`Fee` carry **gross fee, VAT (15%), VDS withheld, AIT/TDS withheld, net cash received** as distinct fields so `Collection Rate`, `Invoice Aging`, `Unbilled Revenue` and `WIP` reflect *real cash*, not gross. Accounting source is pluggable; **the firm's books remain source of truth — on disagreement, the books win and the discrepancy is shown.** | PE | TDS section/rate ambiguity (FY25–26) and edge engagements; residual reduced by surfacing the withholding assumption per invoice for Finance to confirm. |

| 15 | **Low system maturity** — the firm's "system of record" is *principal's head > WhatsApp > email > Excel > paper > ECPS portal*. There is almost nothing structured or queryable to connect to. | 5 | 4 | Accept this as the **design centre, not an edge case**. MVP runs **substantially on manual capture + templated import**, with API connectors as a bonus where they exist. The product's *first job* is to turn the principal's head and the WhatsApp threads into the firm's **first real dataset** — `Decision`, `Approval`, `Change`, effort — then layer analytics. Value is demonstrable from near-zero structured data via `Data Completeness Score` showing the dataset *growing*. | PM/IC | Pilot may stall if the Champion won't invest the initial capture effort; residual managed by making week-1 wins ride on owner-only data the Champion already controls. |
| 16 | **WhatsApp data gap** — the primary channel for decisions/approvals/scope changes has **no history/replay API**; you cannot backfill prior conversations; chats pre-onboarding are unreachable. | 5 | 4 | **No false promise of importing WhatsApp history.** Capture is **forward-only**: a lightweight capture form + (post-MVP) a Cloud-API number where the firm **persists every raw inbound webhook to durable storage before processing**. In the MVP, the realistic path is **manual logging of key WhatsApp decisions into `Decision`/`Approval`/`Change`** (paste/screenshot + structured fields), tagged with source + date + `confidence ≈ 0.6`. Set expectations explicitly: history is gone; capture starts now. | CTO | Decisions made off-platform stay uncaptured unless someone logs them; residual = inherent — mitigated by making logging a 15-second habit, not a chore. |
| 17 | **Internet / power / infra instability** — Dhaka load-shedding, ISP drops, intermittent connectivity break sync and capture mid-flow. | 4 | 3 | **Offline-tolerant capture**: PWA/mobile forms queue locally and sync on reconnect (no data loss on a dropped connection mid-entry). Tally's desktop-bound HTTP server reachability is treated as **intermittent by default** — poll-and-retry, never assume always-on. Region choice (Mumbai/Singapore) minimizes latency. | PE | Severe extended outages still block real-time freshness; residual bounded — queued capture means data is delayed, not lost. |

For *cloud spend*: route hyperscaler USD bills through the offshore entity / international card to avoid forex-clearance delays. For the *firm's* own invoices: multi-currency with FX capture (see #13). ## 4. Top-5 must-not-fail risks and early-warning signals

These are the risks where failure is **existential to the pilot or the company** (Impact 5, or Exposure ≥ 16). For each, we instrument an **early-warning signal** from day one so we react before the failure compounds. The unifying theme: **if the data stops flowing, or starts lying, the product is worthless — so we watch flow and trust above all else.**

| Rank | Must-not-fail risk | Why existential | Early-warning signal (instrumented) | Trigger threshold → action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **#9 AI hallucinations** | One confident, false claim the owner acts on (a fabricated margin or a missed-approval that wasn't missed) **permanently destroys trust** — the entire value prop is "numbers you can trust." Unrecoverable. | Citation-validation failures (claims whose source IDs don't resolve); rate of detector outputs the LLM narrated **without** a backing gold row; Champion "this is wrong" flags on the weekly report. | Any uncited/invalidated claim in a shipped report → **halt AI narration, fall back to raw KPI tiles, root-cause before next send.** |
| 2 | **#2 Missing timesheets → no labour cost** | Without effort data, the firm's #1 pain (per-project profitability, `Forecast Project Margin`, `Utilization Rate`) is unanswerable. If we can't create this primitive, the People domain and half of Money never materialize. | Weekly effort-capture coverage = (Employees who logged ≥1 entry) ÷ (active Employees); trend of `Planned vs Actual Hours` completeness per `Project`. | Coverage < 50% for 2 consecutive weeks → **PM/IC intervention: simplify the form, re-brief via Champion, re-frame the "why."** |
| 3 | **#6 Employee resistance / adoption collapse** | A pilot with no users is a dead pilot regardless of how good the tech is. Adoption is the actual product risk. | Weekly active users by role; capture submissions/week; days-since-last-login per pilot user; "Why this number?" drill-down usage (a proxy for trust/engagement). | Active users trend down 2 weeks running, **or** Owner login gap > 7 days → **CEO/Founder + PM/IC value-review with the Champion.** |
| 4 | **#10 Integration failures (silent staleness)** | Stale-but-pretty dashboards are *worse than no dashboards* — the owner makes real decisions on old data and blames the product when reality diverges. Silent failure is the killer. | Per-`DataSource` heartbeat / "last synced" age; webhook dead-letter queue depth; finance/manual-capture source freshness vs SLA; `Data Completeness Score` sudden drops. | Any critical source (finance, manual capture, WhatsApp persistence) past freshness SLA → **amber tiles + alert to PM/IC + Champion within the hour.** |
| 5 | **#19 Founder / key-person dependency** | A 2–3 person team plus a single Champion is the real fragility. Either side disengaging mid-pilot ends it; this risk silently amplifies #2/#3/#6. | Champion engagement (login + capture cadence); breadth of active firm users beyond the Champion; internal: open architecture decisions undocumented; single-owner code areas (bus-factor map). | Champion-only usage (no second active firm user) by week 4, **or** any architecture area with bus-factor = 1 unresolved → **broaden firm adoption / document + cross-train immediately.** |

**Cross-cutting watch:** risks #1 (project-name matching) and #16 (WhatsApp gap) are not on the top-5 only because they are *designed-around* rather than *defended-against* — `ProjectCrossReference` and forward-only capture turn them from threats into measured, visible completeness gaps. If either mitigation is descoped under deadline pressure, both **immediately promote to must-not-fail**, because they silently corrupt every downstream KPI (#1) or quietly leave the firm's most valuable decisions uncaptured (#16).

## 5. Operating cadence for the register

- **Weekly (pilot):** PM/IC reviews the five early-warning signals; any trigger breached is raised in the standup.
- **Monthly:** CTO + CEO/Founder re-score the full register; re-cost connectors against the 2025–26 vendor-change calendar (#5, #21).
- **Pre-productization gate:** re-evaluate #7 (PDPO enforcement ~May 2027), #12 (config surface generalizes beyond one firm), #18/#21 (unit economics at multi-tenant scale) before opening self-serve onboarding.
- **Every register row** is itself a record with provenance — owner, last-reviewed date, and residual-risk trend — so the register practices the product's own discipline: nothing is asserted without a source and a date.
